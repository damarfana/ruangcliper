About RuangClip

Making digital content management simpler.

RuangClip is a content management application designed to support the management and publication of digital content on Facebook Pages.

The application uses authorized Meta APIs to perform supported content management and publishing operations. Access depends on the permissions granted by the user and Meta's platform requirements.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 13, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how RuangClip handles information when users interact with the application.

1. Information We Access

Depending on the permissions granted, RuangClip may access Facebook Page information, access tokens, account-related identifiers, and content information required to provide its features.

2. How We Use Information

Information is used to support application functions, including authorized Facebook Page management and content publishing.

3. Information Sharing

RuangClip does not sell personal information. Information may be processed or transmitted through Meta services when necessary to provide the requested API functionality.

4. Data Security

Appropriate security measures should be applied to protect information and access credentials handled by the application.

5. Data Retention

Information should only be retained for as long as necessary to provide the application's functions or comply with applicable legal obligations.

6. User Rights

Users may request information about their data or request deletion of information associated with their use of RuangClip.

7. Third-Party Services

RuangClip integrates with Meta platforms. The use of Facebook and related services is also subject to Meta's own privacy policies and terms.

8. Contact

For privacy questions or requests, please contact the RuangClip administrator using the contact information registered in the application.

Data Deletion Instructions

RuangClip respects users' rights to request deletion of information associated with their use of the application.

How to Request Data Deletion

  1. Contact the RuangClip application administrator.
  2. Identify your Facebook account and the relevant Page, if applicable.
  3. Explain that you are requesting deletion of your RuangClip-related data.
  4. The administrator will review and process the request.

Processing of Requests

Requests will be reviewed and processed within 30 days after sufficient information is received, subject to applicable legal requirements.

Revoking Application Access

Users can also remove RuangClip's access through their Facebook account settings, where available. Revoking access does not automatically guarantee deletion of previously stored information.

Questions

For data deletion requests, contact the RuangClip administrator through the contact information provided in the application.
