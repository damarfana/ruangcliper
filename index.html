Privacy Policy & Data Deletion

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 13, 2026

RuangClip is an application used to help manage and publish content to Facebook Pages through Meta's authorized APIs.

1. Information We Access

Depending on the permissions granted by the user, RuangClip may access Facebook Page information, Page access tokens, and content information needed to publish or manage posts.

2. How Information Is Used

Information accessed by RuangClip is used only to provide the application's content management and publishing functions.

3. Data Sharing

RuangClip does not sell personal information. Information is shared with Meta only as necessary to provide Facebook API functionality.

4. Data Security

Reasonable measures should be implemented to protect access credentials and information handled by the application.

5. Data Retention

Information is retained only for as long as necessary to provide the application's services or meet applicable legal requirements.

6. Contact

For privacy questions, contact the application administrator through the contact information provided in the application.

Data Deletion Instructions

Users may request deletion of information associated with their use of RuangClip.

How to Request Data Deletion

  1. Send a deletion request to the application administrator.
  2. Include your Facebook account name and relevant details.
  3. Specify that you want your RuangClip-related data deleted.

Requests will be reviewed and processed within 30 days, subject to applicable legal requirements.

Deleting the application or revoking its Facebook permissions can also be done through your Facebook account settings.
