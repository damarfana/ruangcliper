Data Deletion Instructions

RuangClip respects users' rights to request deletion of information associated with their use of the application.

How to Request Data Deletion

  1. Contact the RuangClip application administrator through the email below.
  2. Provide your Facebook account information and relevant details about your use of RuangClip.
  3. Clearly state that you are requesting deletion of your data.
  4. The administrator will verify and process your request.

Processing Time

Deletion requests will be reviewed and processed within 30 days after sufficient information is received, subject to applicable legal requirements.

Revoking Application Access

You can also remove RuangClip's access through your Facebook account settings, where available. Revoking access does not automatically delete previously stored information.
